guitar/, cello/, clarinet/: Philharmonia Orchestra sound samples (https://philharmonia.co.uk/resources/sound-samples/).
Edited for this app: leading silence trimmed, peak-normalised to -1 dBFS, 50 ms fade-out, re-encoded as mono 96 kbps MP3. Guitar notes then cut to their first 3.1 s by copying MP3 frames (no re-encoding): the longest note the app plays is 2 s. All three then re-encoded from those files as mono 48 kbps MP3 (44.1 kHz): no audible difference in a listening comparison, about half the bytes.
