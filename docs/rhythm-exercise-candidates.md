# Rhythm exercise candidates

Selection list only; no implementation decisions yet. Duplicates and equivalent variants are merged.

The agreed selection and ongoing exercise design are in [Rhythm exercises plan](rhythm-exercises-plan.md).

## Ideas already in the app docs

Sources: [Ear-training landscape](ear-training-landscape.md) and [EarMaster research](earmaster.md).

| Exercise | Short description |
|---|---|
| Rhythm reading / sight-reading | Tap or clap a written rhythm in time, including rhythms from familiar tunes. |
| Rhythm clap-back / tap-back | Hear a rhythm, then reproduce it from memory. |
| Rhythm dictation | Write the rhythm you hear using notes and rests. |
| Rhythm error detection | Find where a played rhythm differs from the displayed score. |
| Heard-change detection | Hear a phrase twice; identify whether pitch, rhythm, or nothing changed. |
| Pulse tapping | Tap the steady beat of music or a metronome. |
| Skipping beats | Keep time while deliberately leaving selected beats untapped. |
| Metre identification | Identify the beat grouping or time signature by listening. |
| [Rhythm recognition / puzzles](https://trainer.thetamusic.com/en) | Choose the written rhythm matching what you hear. |
| [Drum-style identification](https://trainer.thetamusic.com/en) | Recognize rhythmic styles from drum patterns. |
| Melodic dictation with rhythm | Write both the pitches and rhythm of a heard melody. |

Rhythm recognition/puzzles and drum-style identification appear in the landscape's research notes rather than its formal exercise catalogue. Beginner beat dictation/error detection and combined pulse/metre tests are included in their corresponding rows above.

## Additional candidates from online research

These are not explicitly listed as exercises in either document. Links point to the source of each idea; suitability for Polyhymnia is our assessment. Research checked 2026-10-04.

| Exercise | Short description |
|---|---|
| [Silent-bar timing](https://www.soundbrenner.com/blogs/articles/5-metronome-exercises-build-internal-clock) | Continue tapping through silent bars; check alignment when the click returns. |
| [Displaced-click timing](https://www.soundbrenner.com/blogs/articles/5-metronome-exercises-build-internal-clock) | Keep the main beat while the metronome clicks on backbeats or offbeats. |
| [Subdivision switching](https://piano.samesound.ru/en/practice/rhythm) | Switch between eighths, triplets, and sixteenths without changing tempo. |
| [Polyrhythm coordination](https://learnmusictheory.net/rhythm/) | Tap two simultaneous patterns, such as two against three. |
